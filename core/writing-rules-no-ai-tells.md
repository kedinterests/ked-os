# Writing Rules: No AI Tells

**Hard rule. Applies to every piece of content Claude drafts in KED-OS, for Kenny or Chris, in any domain:** client copy, proposals, emails, site content, documentation, social, support replies, anything. Not optional, not project-specific.

Source: "AI Watermarking & Avoiding AI Tells" (AI Works library, added 2026-09-16). Anthropic's model-level watermark is invisible and only Anthropic can read it. The real exposure is third-party detectors (Copyleaks, Originality.ai, GPTZero, Turnitin) that pattern-match on stylistic tells. These rules defend against that, and they also just make the writing better.

The same rule is in force in life-os and Brilliant OS. Where the three copies overlap, they agree.

---

## Structure

- No em dashes (—) or en dashes (–). Use commas, colons, semicolons, periods, or parentheses. (Already a global ban.)
- No "it's not X, it's Y" or "this isn't about X, it's about Y" constructions.
- No throat-clearing openers: "In today's world," "In a world where," "As we navigate," or similar.
- No closing paragraph that restates what was already said. End on a real point.
- No reflexive rule-of-three lists (three adjectives, three examples, three clauses) when one or two would do.
- Vary sentence length and rhythm. Do not fall into a pattern of short-punchy-sentence after every longer sentence.
- No zero-padded designed numerals ("01, 02, 03", "No. 01"). Write 1, 2, 3. Applies to mockups and designed pages, not just prose.

## Word choice

- Banned: delve, tapestry, landscape (as metaphor), realm, unlock, unleash, elevate, journey (as metaphor), quietly (figurative), profound, testament to, underscores, boasts, seamless, robust, leverage (as a verb), game-changer, in the ever-evolving, myriad, paramount, nestled.
- No hedge-stacking: "it's worth noting that," "it's important to remember," "arguably," "in many ways" used more than once in a piece.
- No stacked transitional signposting: "Furthermore," "Moreover," "Additionally," "That said," one after another.
- No atmospheric filler: "here's the thing," "to be clear," "honestly." Say the thing.

## Tone

- Do not narrate reasoning or helpfulness ("I hope this helps," "let's dive in," "great question").
- Do not over-qualify claims into mush. State things plainly when they are plain.
- Write like a specific person with an opinion, not like a balanced summary of every possible view.
- Do not invent a term and then use it as established shorthand. If Kenny would not have heard the phrase before this conversation, say the plain thing instead.

---

## Enforcement

Before handing any draft to Kenny or Chris, scan it against this list. If it has three or more violations, revise first. Do not ask whether to revise. Just revise.

For anything reputation-critical (published site content, client-facing copy, anything under the KED name or a client's name), flag that a final human pass is still the reliable move. No prompt eliminates every statistical tell.
