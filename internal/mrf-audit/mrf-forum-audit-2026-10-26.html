Findings

What the audit turned up

Ranked by how much they matter to members, advertisers and search traffic. Red items should be handled before more members opt in to the new design.

High

The new theme is missing almost everything the classic theme carries

"MRF 2026" is already user-selectable, and the opt-in banner is attached to the classic theme, so members can switch today. The classic theme (Default, Mar 2024) has 26 components attached. MRF 2026 has one: the opt-in banner. A member who switches loses these, among others:

  • Business Member Signature and Industry Pros Topic Redirects, which paying business members see the value of.
  • Rotate on Refresh Global Banner (kedinterests repo). If banner ads run through it, opted-in members stop seeing them. Ads served by the ad plugin itself should carry over, since that runs at the server level.
  • Custom CSS and Header-Footer Scripts, plus Signup Banner, Social Share, Follow Category Button, Custom Header Links and Like Column.

The proposal promised advertisers and members that nothing changes. Right now that only holds for people who stay on classic.

High

The hero stats on the new theme are wrong

MRF 2026 shows 47,000+ members and 185,000+ posts. The forum's own counts today are 18,652 accounts and 395,893 posts. The member figure is overstated by about 2.5 times, and the post figure undersells the archive by half. The same numbers appear in the redesign proposal notes. Unless the 47,000 comes from a source outside Discourse (pre-migration accounts, newsletter list), it should change before anyone else sees it.

High

Two redesign builds exist, and only one is reachable

"MRF 2026" is a local theme with no git source. Separately, a component called "MineralRightsForum Redesign 2026" syncs from kedinterests/mrf-rebuild-2026, but it is attached to the system Horizon theme, which no member can select. One build should become the source of truth, kept in git, and the other retired.

Medium

Every first page load ships all 801 categories

The homepage HTML is 2.4 MB before compression (134 KB over the wire with brotli). The site payload behind it is about 2 MB, and nearly all of that is category data. Phones on rural connections, which describes a lot of mineral owners, have to download and parse it before the page becomes usable. Discourse has a lazy category loading option built for forums with hundreds of categories.

Medium

Category sprawl is thinning out the site

801 categories, 740 of them subcategories (mostly counties). 503 have 10 topics or fewer and 279 have 2 or fewer. 24 of the 60 top-level categories got no new topic in the past year. For Google these are thin pages. For members they make it harder to know where to post. Tags are currently off, and state, county, operator and basin tags could carry much of what the subcategories do now.

This is a strategy call for Kenny. County pages may be pulling search traffic that justifies keeping them, and Search Console data would settle it.

Medium

Questions are going unanswered

9 of the last 100 topics have no replies. Several are newcomer questions with vague titles: "Royalties from mineral rights" (44 views), "Leases and expenses" (72 views), "Schedule A, Oil and Gas Lease Grady Co, Ok" (48 views). A first question that gets silence is usually the last one that person asks. Only 258 of 1,277 active users posted in the last month, and likes are low (267 in 30 days), so a handful of regulars carry the answering load.

Medium

Typos in first posts end up in Google results

Discourse builds each topic's meta description from the first post. Yesterday's "Royalties from mineral rights" topic includes "and ard trying to find out," and that typo is in the snippet Google will show. Fixing the first post and title of each new topic improves the search listing as well as the page.

Low

Theme housekeeping

  • Official: Developer Toolbox is enabled on the production theme. It is a development utility and should come off.
  • Five disabled components are still attached to the classic theme: Analytics Scripts, Add View My Business Profile, Banner Featured Links, Group Signatures Component, Search Banner.
  • "Redirect Email Advertisers" is enabled but attached to no theme, so it does nothing. Either it should be attached or removed.
Good

The technical SEO basics are in place

Canonical tags and meta descriptions on every page. Topic pages carry DiscussionForumPosting, BreadcrumbList and Organization schema. The sitemap index is live and updated today. HTTP and the bare domain both 301 to https://www.. HTTP/3 and brotli are on through Cloudflare. Discourse is on the current release (2026.9.1), and every git-backed component is up to date.

Not measured

Gaps in this audit

  • Core Web Vitals: PageSpeed Insights refused the request because the shared daily quota was used up. It needs a free API key or a manual run.
  • Traffic: the Google Analytics connector needs reauthorizing, and Search Console for mineralrightsforum.com is not connected yet.
  • Restricted content: the post sample covers public categories only.

Editorial plan

Keeping posts clean without rewriting members

The forum runs about 34 posts a day, which is easy to review completely. Spelling turns out to be the smallest part of the job. The bigger wins are vague titles, unanswered questions, and members accidentally posting private information. The plan works in three layers, cheapest first.

1

Catch it before it posts

  • Category topic templates that ask for county, state, acreage, operator and the question.
  • Title placeholder showing what a good title looks like.
  • Title prettify for caps and punctuation.
  • Discourse AI composer proofreading, if the hosting includes it, so members correct their own text.
2

Review every morning

  • A scheduled run at 6 AM Central reads every post from the previous day.
  • It produces a one-page report: what to fix, what needs an answer, what needs a moderator.
  • Nothing changes on the forum at this stage. It is a report.
3

Approve, then apply

  • You tick the fixes you agree with.
  • The editor account applies them with an edit reason like "Typo fix (staff)".
  • After a month of clean results, trivial typo fixes can go automatic and only the judgment calls come to you.

The daily report

What lands in your inbox each morning

Needs an answer. Topics with no reply after 12 hours, with the regulars who most often answer in that county or category, so you can tag them.
Title rewrites. Vague titles with a specific replacement. "Leases and expenses" becomes something a searcher would type.
Spelling and grammar. Clear errors only, shown as a before and after, one checkbox each.
Privacy flags. Phone numbers, home addresses, owner numbers, account numbers, and screenshots of check stubs or division orders that show names. These get hidden fast, before Google indexes them.
Solicitation and spam. Unsolicited purchase offers, new accounts posting phone numbers or links, buyers posing as owners.
First-time posters. New members to welcome personally, since a reply on a first post is what brings people back.
Heated threads. Conversations drifting toward personal attacks, before they need a lock.
Signals for Kenny. Counties, operators and questions trending this week, for the newsletter, MineralWise topics and advertiser pitches.

Daily, real time, or both?

Daily batch only

Start here

One scheduled run each morning. Simple, cheap, easy to trust, and a single place to review. A typo sitting for a day costs almost nothing.

Weak spot: a posted Social Security number or phone number stays up until morning.

Hybrid

Recommended

The daily report handles everything editorial. A Discourse webhook fires on each new post and sends it to a small Cloudflare Worker that checks only for privacy exposure and spam, then alerts staff within a minute or two.

Urgent items move fast. Everything else waits for the calm morning review.

Real time for everything

Not yet

Each post checked and corrected as it lands. Fastest, but members watching their posts change minutes after publishing feels like surveillance, and mistakes reach the forum before anyone reviews them.

Running cost at about 1,000 posts a month is small: low tens of dollars a month at most in model usage, plus Cloudflare's free tier for the Worker.

Editing rules

What staff edits may and may not touch

On a forum where people post lease terms, decimal interests and legal questions, a "small" edit can change what someone said. These rules keep edits safe. Kenny should approve them before any edit goes live.

  • Titles: may be rewritten for clarity and search, keeping the poster's question.
  • Spelling: clear misspellings only ("ard" to "are").
  • Company and place names: capitalize correctly (Continental, Grady County, OK).
  • Never change numbers, dollar amounts, percentages, dates, legal descriptions, or section, township and range.
  • Never change opinion, tone, slang or regional phrasing. "Til" in a reply can stay.
  • Replies get a lighter touch than first posts. Most need nothing.
  • Every edit carries an edit reason and is made by the editor account, never silently.
  • Privacy edits (removing a phone number or SSN) take priority over everything and can go out without the morning batch.
