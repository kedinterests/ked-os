Plains Exploration and Production Co.
Plains Exploration is an oil and gas exploration and production company with headquarters in Houston, Texas. The Company's primary areas of operation are in California, the Gulf Coast region, the Mid-continent region, and the Rocky Mountains. Plains Exploration's stock is listed on the NASDAQ as PXP.
