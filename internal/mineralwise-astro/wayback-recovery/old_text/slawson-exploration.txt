Slawson Exploration
Slawson Exploration is an oil and gas exploration and production company based in Wichita Kansas. Slawson has drilled over 3,500 wells. Slawson Exploration focuses on the Northern Rockies, California, the Anadarko Basin, Gulf Coast, South Texas, and Western Kansas.
Contact Information for Royalty Owners - Tel. (713) 759-1311
Those who own gas royalties and mineral royalties are an important group to Slawson Exploration. If you receive oil royalty or gas royalty from a lease under production by Slawson Exploration, you can contact them at (713) 759-1311.
