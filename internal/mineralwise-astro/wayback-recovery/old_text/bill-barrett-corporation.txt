Bill Barrett Corporation
Bill Barrett Corp. is an Oil Company specializing in natural gas and oil exploration and development near the Rocky Mountains. Bill Barrett Corp. was founded in 2002 by William Barrett, and has headquarters in Denver, Colorado. The company went public in 2004 and is listed in the NYSE as BBG. Bill Barrett Corp is most well known for its discovery of natural gas reserves in the West Tavaputs plateau in Utah.
Contact Information for Royalty Owners - Tel. (303) 293-9100
