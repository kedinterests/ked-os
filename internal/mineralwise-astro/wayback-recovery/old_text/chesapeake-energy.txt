Chesapeake Energy
Chesapeake Energy is the most active driller of new wells in the United States and one of the nation's largest producers of natural gas. Chesapeake stock is traded on the NYSE under the symbol CHK. Chesapeake Energy focuses on drilling and developing onshore U.S. natural gas reserves in north-central Texas ( Barnett Shale ), East Texas ( Haynesville Shale ), Louisiana ( Fayetteville Shale ), and the Appalachian Basin ( Marcellus Shale). Chesapeake Energy also actively drills in other areas including the Mid-Continent , Appalachian Basin, Permian Basin, South Texas, Gulf Coast, and East Texas.
Contact Information for Royalty Owners - Tel. (877) CHK-1GAS
Those who own gas royalties and oil royalties are an important group to Chesapeake Energy. If you receive oil royalty or gas royalty from a lease under production by Chesapeake Energy, you can contact them at (877) CHK-1GAS.
