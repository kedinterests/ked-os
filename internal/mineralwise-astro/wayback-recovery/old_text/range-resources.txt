Range Resources
Range Resources is an oil and gas company that operates in the Southwest, Appalachian Basin and the Gulf Coast. Range Resources stock is listed on the New York Stock Exchange under the symbol RRC. Range focuses on natural gas shale and coal bed methane projects. The 2008 year-end drilling inventory for the oil and gas drilling company included more than 12,000 proven drilling projects, and 3.7 (3.0 net) million acres of leases with proved reserves totaling 2.7 Tcfe.
Contact Information for Royalty Owners - Tel. 1-877-506-4133
Those who own gas royalties and mineral royalties are an important group to Range Resources. If you receive oil royalty or gas royalty from a lease under production by Range Resources, you can contact them at the Royalty Owner Relations Administrator: 1-877-506-4133, Southwestern Pennsylvania Marcellus Shale 724-743-6700 and the North Texas Barnett Shale 817-870-2601.
