Murex Petroleum
Murex Petroleum is an oil and gas exploration and production company based in Houston Texas. Murex Petroleum operates in North Dakota, South Dakota, and the Rocky Mountain Region. Murex Petroleum also has non operating working interests in North Dakota, Montana, and Oklahoma.
Contact Information for Royalty Owners - Tel. (281) 590-3313
Those who own gas royalties and oil royalties are an important group to Murex Petroleum. If you receive oil royalty or gas royalty from a lease under production by Murex Petroleum, you can contact them at (281) 590-3313.
