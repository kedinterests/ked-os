Stone Energy
Stone Energy is an oil and gas exploration and production company with headquarters in Lafayette, Louisiana. The Company's primary operations are in West Virginia and Texas. Stone Energy's stock can be found listed on the NYSE as SGY.
Contact Information for Royalty Owners - Tel. (337) 237-0410
Those who own gas royalties and oil royalties are an important group to Stone Energy. If you receive oil royalty or gas royalty from a lease under production by Stone Energy, you can contact them at (337) 237-0410.
