SandRidge Energy, Inc.
SandRidge Energy is an oil and gas production and exploration company with headquarters in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma. The Company operates in West Texas, the Mid-Continent, the Permian Basin the Gulf Coast, and the Cotton Valley Trend East Texas. SandRidge Energy is listed on the NYSE as SD.
Contact Information for Royalty Owners - Tel. (405) 429-5500
Those who own gas royalties and oil royalties are an important group to SandRidge Energy. If you receive oil royalty or gas royalty from a lease under production by SandRidge Energy, you can contact them at (405) 429-5500.
