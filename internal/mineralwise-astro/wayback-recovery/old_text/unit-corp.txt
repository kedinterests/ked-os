Unit Corp.
Unit Corp is an oil and gas exploration and production company with headquarters in Tulsa, Oklahoma. The Company’s primary operations occur in the Arkhoma and Anadarko Basins, the Rocky Mountains, and the Louisiana and Texas Gulf Coast. The Company was founded in 1963 and its stock is listed on the NYSE as UDE.
