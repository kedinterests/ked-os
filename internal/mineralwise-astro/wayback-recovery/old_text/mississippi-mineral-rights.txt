Mississippi Mineral Rights
Mississippi Mineral Rights pertaining to oil and gas have been productive since October of 1926 with the drilling of the Amory Petroleum Company #1 Carter. Mississippi minerals also include a wide range of hydrocarbon products including crude oil, natural gas, NGL's, CO2, and coal. The first field discovered in Mississippi was the Amory Field in Monroe County. The top counties by well count are Jasper County, Jefferson Davis County, and Lamar County.
