Comstock Resources
Comstock Resources is an independent energy company based in Frisco, TX. Comstock stock is traded on the NYSE under the symbol CRK. The company focuses its drilling operations in the East Texas / North Louisiana and South Texas regions. Comstock has acquired over 984 Bcfe (billion cubic feet equivalent) of proved oil and gas reserves since 1991. 90% of these reserves are natural gas while the remaining 10% are crude oil. Comstock Resources also drills in the Haynesville Shale. The company recently sold their offshore oil and gas operations to Stone Energy Corporation.
