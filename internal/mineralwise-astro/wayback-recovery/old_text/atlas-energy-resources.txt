Atlas Energy Resources
Atlas Energy Resources is one of the largest independent natural gas producers in the Eastern United States. Atlas Energy stock and its subsidiaries are traded on the NYSE under the symbols ATN, ATLS, APL, and AHD. Atlas Energy's oil and gas drilling operations focus on the Appalachian Basin and northern Michigan's Antrim Basin. The company operates more than 7,600 oil and gas wells in Pennsylvania, Ohio, Tennessee and New York.
