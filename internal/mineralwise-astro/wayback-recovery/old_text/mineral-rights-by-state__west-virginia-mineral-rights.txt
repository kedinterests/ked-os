West Virginia Mineral Rights
West Virginia Mineral Rights have a history that goes back to the time when Indians primarily occupied the land. Native Americans called natural gas vents "burning springs". The oil and gas industry in West Virginia got its start as a result of salt mining. Many large oil and gas fields were found near to or over the top of salt domes. The Rathbone brothers discovered the first oil well in 1859.
