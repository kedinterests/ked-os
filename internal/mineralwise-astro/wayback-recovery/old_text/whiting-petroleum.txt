Whiting Petroleum
Whiting Petroleum is an oil and gas exploration and production company based in Denver Colorado. Whiting Petroleum stock is listed on the NYSE under the symbol WLL. Whiting Petroleum focuses its exploration and production activities on the following areas: the Permian Basin of west Texas and New Mexico, the Rocky Mountains, the Mid-Continent area, and the Gulf Coast, and Michigan Basins.
Contact Information for Royalty Owners - Tel. (303) 837-1661
Those who own gas royalties and mineral royalties are an important group to Whiting Petroleum. If you receive oil royalty or gas royalty from a lease under production by Whiting Petroleum, you can contact them at (303) 837-1661.
