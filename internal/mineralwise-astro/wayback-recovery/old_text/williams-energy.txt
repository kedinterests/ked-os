Williams Energy
Williams Energy is a natural gas exploration and production company with headquarters in Tulsa, Oklahoma. The Company's primary areas of operation include the Piceance, Paradox, and San Juan Basins; the Arkoma Basin; the Marcellus and Barnett shales. Williams Energy's stock is listed on the NYSE as WMB.
Contact Information for Royalty Owners - Tel. (800) 945-5426
Those who own gas royalties and oil royalties are an important group to Williams. If you receive oil royalty or gas royalty from a lease under production by Williams, you can contact them at (800) 945-5426.
