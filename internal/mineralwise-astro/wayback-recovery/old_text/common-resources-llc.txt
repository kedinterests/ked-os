Common Resources, L.L.C.
Common Resources in an oil and gas exploration and production company with headquarters in The Woodlands, Texas. The Company's primary areas of operation include West Texas, The Gulf Coast, and the Mid-continent region.

Contact Information for Royalty Owners - Tel. (281) 363-9200
Those who own gas royalties and oil royalties are an important group to Common Resources. If you receive oil royalty or gas royalty from a lease under production by Common Resources, you can contact them at (281) 363-9200
