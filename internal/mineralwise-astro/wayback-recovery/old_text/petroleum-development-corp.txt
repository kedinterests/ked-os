Petroleum Development Corp.
Petroleum Development Corp. is an independent oil and gas exploration and production company with headquarters in Denver, Colorado. PDC's stock is listed on the NASDAQ-GS as PETD. The Company's operations are primarily in the Appalachian Basin and Rocky Mountain regions. Founded in 1969, over 80% of the PDC Energy's production comes from natural gas. The Company has proved reserves of 720 Bcfe.
Contact Information for Royalty Owners - Tel. (303) 860-5800
