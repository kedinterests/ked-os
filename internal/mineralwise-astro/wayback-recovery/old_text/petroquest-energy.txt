PetroQuest Energy
PetroQuest Energy is an oil and gas exploration and production company based in the United States. PetroQuest stock is publicly traded and listed on the NYSE under the symbol PQ. PetroQuest focuses on the following geographic areas for oil and natural gas production: East Texas, Arkoma Basin, South Louisiana, and the Gulf of Mexico.
PetroQuest is also active in the Woodford and Fayetteville shale plays of the Arkoma Basin. Approximately 75% of PetroQuest's budget is devoted to onshore operations.
