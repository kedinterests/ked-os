Mariner Energy, Inc.
Mariner Energy is an oil and gas exploration and production company with headquarters in Houston, Texas. The Company's stock is listed on the NYSE as ME. The Company's operations are primarily in the Gulf Coast and Permian Basin regions. The Company has over 1 trillion cubic feet in proven reserves.
Contact Information for Royalty Owners - Tel. (713) 358-8639
Those who own gas royalties and oil royalties are an important group to Mariner Energy. If you receive oil royalty or gas royalty from a lease under production by Mariner Energy, you can contact them at (713) 209-2400.
