Colorado Mineral Rights
Colorado Mineral Rights have wide ranging values. Colorado's mineral rights cover a wide range of hydrocarbon products including crude oil, natural gas, gold, silver, and coal. The first oil field in Colorado was the Florence oil field discovered in the late 1800's.
