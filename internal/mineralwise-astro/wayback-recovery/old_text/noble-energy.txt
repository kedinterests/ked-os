Noble Energy
Noble Energy is an oil and gas exploration and production company based in the U.S. Noble stock is listed on the NYSE under the symbol NBL. Noble Energy focuses on low risk development projects in the Rocky Mountains, Mid-Continent area, and the Gulf Coast. Noble's largest natural gas asset is the Wattenberg field located in eastern Colorado. Noble also has several large positions in natural gas shale plays across the country.
Contact Information for Royalty Owners - Tel. 1-(800) 220-5824
