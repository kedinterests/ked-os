Rosetta Resources, Inc.
Rosetta Resources is an oil and gas exploration and production company with headquarters in Houston, Texas. The Company's primary areas of operation are in South Texas, the San Juan Basin of New Mexico, the DJ Basin of Colorado, the Sacramento Basin of California, and the Alberta Basin of Montana. Rosetta's stock is listed on the NASDAQ as ROSE.
Contact Information for Royalty Owners - Tel. 1-800-526-2112
Those who own gas royalties and oil royalties are an important group to Rosetta Resources. If you receive oil royalty or gas royalty from a lease under production by Rosetta Resources, you can contact them at 1-800-526-2112.
