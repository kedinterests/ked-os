North Dakota Mineral Rights
Oil was first discovered in North Dakota in 1951. Mineral rights in this sparsely populated region bordering Canada did not receive much attention until the discovery of the Bakken Shale formation. The Bakken Shale formation spreads over 24,000 square miles. Most of the mineral rights owners in North Dakota are farmers who have been tilling the soil for generations.
