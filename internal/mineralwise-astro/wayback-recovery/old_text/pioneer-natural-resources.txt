Pioneer Natural Resources
Pioneer Natural Resources is a large independent oil and gas exploration and production company. Pioneer stock trades on the NYSE under the symbol PXD. Pioneer Natural produces oil and gas in multiple operating areas including: the West Texas Spraberry, the South Texas Edwards and Eagle Ford shale, the Barnett shale of North Texas, Alaska, as well as international locations. Pioneer is also pursuing several emerging resource plays in the Lower 48. Pioneer has international operations in Tunisia and South Africa.
Contact Information for Royalty Owners - Tel. (972) 444-9001
Those who own gas royalties and mineral royalties are an important group to Pioneer Natural Resources. If you receive oil royalty or gas royalty from a lease under production by Pioneer Natural Resources, you can contact them at (972) 444-9001.
