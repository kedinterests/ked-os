Merit Energy
Merit Energy is an oil and gas exploration and production company with headquarters in Dallas, Texas. Founded in 1989, the Company has proved reserves of over 450 MMBOE and operates primarily in the southern U.S. states of Texas, Louisiana, New Mexico, and Oklahoma.
Contact Information for Royalty Owners - Tel. (972) 628-1511
Those who own gas royalties and oil royalties are an important group to Merit Energy. If you receive oil royalty or gas royalty from a lease under production by Merit Energy, you can contact them at (972) 628-1511.
