Anschutz Exploration Corp.
Anschutz Exploration is a privately-held oil and gas exploration and production company with headquarters in Denver, Colorado. The Company’s primary areas of operation include New York, Pennsylvania, Ohio, North Dakota, Montana, and Wyoming. Anschutz was founded in 1965.
