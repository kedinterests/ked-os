Dominion Resources Inc.
Dominion Resources exited the producing business when it closed the sale of its natural gas exploration and production business on April 30, 2010 to a subsidiary of CONSOL Energy. The transaction included rights to about 490,000 acres in the Marcellus shale producing region of Pennsylvania and West Virginia. Exiting the E&P business enhances the visibility of Dominion's core natural gas pipeline and storage businesses. Activities in the Appalachian region will now focus on investments such as the new gathering and pipeline infrastructure that development of the Marcellus formation will demand. Dominion owns and operates the nation's largest underground natural gas storage system with about 942 billion cubic feet of storage capacity. The Company's stock is listed on the NYSE as D ( Common Stock).
