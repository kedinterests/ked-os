Marcellus Shale West Virginia
The Marcellus Shale covers the north central and lower portion of the eastern panhandle of West Virginia.

Marcellus Shale Geology
The Marcellus shale is from the Middle Devonian age and is located between 5,000 and 8,000 feet below the surface.
