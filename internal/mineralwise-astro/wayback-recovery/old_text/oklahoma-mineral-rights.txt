Oklahoma Mineral Rights
Oklahoma Mineral Rights cover some of the most resource rich areas in the United States. Oklahoma contains over 7,500 productive formation names. Oklahoma ranks 5th in the nation for crude oil production. Oklahoma accounts for about 8% of the total U.S. natural gas production. Over 480,000 wells have been drilled in the state of Oklahoma, and at one time in 1927 it was the largest oil producing region in the world. The first oil well discovered in Oklahoma was the Nellie Johnstone No. 1 in 1897.
