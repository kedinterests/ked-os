Ultra Petroleum
Ultra Petroleum is an oil and gas exploration and production company with headquarters in Houston, Texas. Ultra Petroleum's primary areas of operation are in the Green River Basin in Wyoming, and the Appalachian Basin. The Company's stock can be found listed on the NYSE as UPL.
Contact Information for Royalty Owners - Tel. (281) 876-0120
Those who own gas royalties and oil royalties are an important group to Ultra Petroleum. If you receive oil royalty or gas royalty from a lease under production by Ultra Petroleum, you can contact them at (281) 876-0120.
