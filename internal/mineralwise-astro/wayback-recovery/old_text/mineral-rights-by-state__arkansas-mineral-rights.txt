Arkansas Mineral Rights
Arkansas Mineral Rights produce the most economic impact to the state through the production of natural gas minerals. In recent years, the Fayetteville Shale has become a major play in the Arkansas mineral rights arena. Because of the advances in horizontal drilling technology and hydraulic fracturing, Arkansas mineral rights covering the Fayetville Shale are now economically viable.
