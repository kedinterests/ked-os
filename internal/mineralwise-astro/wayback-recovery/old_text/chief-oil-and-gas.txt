Chief Oil & Gas
Chief Oil & Gas is a privately held independent oil and gas company. Chief Oil & Gas drills and operates in the Appalachian Basin, northern Texas, and central Utah. The company also holds over 500,000 acres in the Marcellus Shale region of New York, Pennsylvania, Ohio, Maryland, and West Virginia.
Contact Information for Royalty Owners - Tel. (214) 265-9590
Those who own gas royalties and oil royalties are an important group to Chief Oil & Gas. If you receive oil royalty or gas royalty from a lease under production by Chief Oil & Gas, you can contact them at (214) 265-9590.
