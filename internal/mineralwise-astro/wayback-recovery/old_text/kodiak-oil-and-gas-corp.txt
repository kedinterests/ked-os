Kodiak Oil & Gas Corp.
Kodiak Oil & Gas is an independent oil & gas exploration and production company with headquarters in Denver, Colorado. The Company's primary area of operation is in the Rocky Mountain region of the U.S. It also operates in parts of Montana and North Dakota. Kodiak's stock is listed in the NYSE as KOG.
Contact Information for Royalty Owners - Tel. (303) 592-8075
Those who own gas royalties and oil royalties are an important group to Kodiak Oil & Gas Corp. If you receive oil royalty or gas royalty from a lease under production by Kodiak Oil & Gas, you can contact them at (303) 592-8075.
