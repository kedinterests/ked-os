Swift Energy Operating, L.L.C.
Swift Energy is a American oil and gas exploration and production company with headquarters in Houston, Texas. The Company was founded in 1979 by Aubrey Swift. Swift Energy's stock is listed on the XNYS as SFY. The Company's primary areas of operation are in South Texas, Southeast Louisiana, South Louisiana, and Central Louisiana/East Texas.
Contact Information for Royalty Owners - Tel. (281) 874-2700
Those who own gas royalties and oil royalties are an important group to Swift Energy. If you receive oil royalty or gas royalty from a lease under production by Swift Energy, you can contact them at (281) 874-2700
