Huron Shale
The Huron Shale is located in the Appalachian Basin in West Virginia and Kentucky.
Huron Shale Companies
Significant producers within the Huron Shale are:
EQT Corp.
Equitable Resources
Exco (XCO) Resources
Range Resources.
Huron Shale Geology
The Huron Shale is a geological formation ranging from 200 to 2,000 feet thick and is found at depths of 4,000 to 5,000 feet.
