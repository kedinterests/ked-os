Energen Corp.
Energen Corporation is an American oil and gas company with headquarters in Birmingham, Alabama. The Company's stock is listed in the NYSE as EGN. Energen operates primarily in northern and central Alabama, but also has operations in the Permian Basin region.
Contact Information for Royalty Owners - Tel. (205) 326-2700
Those who own gas royalties and oil royalties are an important group to Energen Corp. If you receive oil royalty or gas royalty from a lease under production by Energen Corp., you can contact them at (205) 326-2700 .
