## What the 20 August diagnosis got right, and what it got wrong

Right: the cause is the migration, not an algorithm change. Every engine fell
together, and the mechanism is 235 old URLs returning 404.

Wrong on two counts, both worth knowing before anyone acts on that document:

- **"About 120 glossary pages have no internal links."** Checked directly: there
  are 199 term pages and the A-Z index links all 199. The real orphans were the
  26 empty alphabet stub pages, which nothing linked to and which existed only in
  the sitemap. Those are now deleted.
- **"301 `/what-is-fracking/` into the glossary entry."** `/what-is-fracking/` is
  a 10 KB article; the glossary entry is 40 words. Merging the strong page into
  the weak one is backwards. Kept the article as is.

## What was actually still broken

1. `_redirects` wildcards redirected into pages that were never built. The
   migration renamed slugs, so `/oil-and-gas-terms/fracking/` 301'd to
   `/resources/oil-and-gas-terms/fracking/`, which 404s. Content was at
   `fracking-hydraulic-fracturing-fracture-fracing-frac-job` all along.
2. `_redirects` never matched a URL without a trailing slash. Google holds both
   variants from the Squarespace era, so roughly half the inventory died.
3. www redirected on the homepage only. Deep www URLs returned 200 — a complete
   duplicate of the site on a second host. Nobody had checked past `/`.
4. The Squarespace-era nested paths (`/owners-guide/...`, `/library/...`,
   `/directory/...`) had no redirect map at all.

## What was done

- All redirects moved into `functions/_middleware.js`, generated by
  `scripts/build-redirects.mjs`. The generator resolves every URL in
  `scripts/legacy-urls.txt` against the pages that exist in `src/pages` and
  **refuses to emit a rule pointing at a missing page**.
- `scripts/legacy-urls.txt`: 909 historical URLs, reconstructed from the Wayback
  CDX index plus the pre-migration sitemap scrape. This stands in for the GSC
  404 export, which was not reachable programmatically (mineralwise.com is not in
  either connected Search Console account).
- 472 of those 909 now redirect; the rest are either live pages or genuinely dead
  URLs left to 404 on purpose. No catch-all to the homepage.
- www consolidation moved into code, path and query preserved.
- `_redirects` cut from 20 rules to 2.
- Deleted the 26 alphabet stubs and the duplicate fracking term page.
- `scripts/test-redirects.mjs` guards all of it.
